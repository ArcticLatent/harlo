Built like a desktop app, because it is one

Many AI agent apps are web pages in an Electron window. Harlo is Rust from top to bottom, the window and the server that runs your agents alike, so it starts at once and sits quiet.

All Rust
The window, the server that runs your agents and its engine are compiled Rust. Long sessions don’t slow the window down.
Drawn by your GPU
The interface is drawn by Slint, with a software renderer to fall back on. No HTML, no JavaScript framework, no bundled Chromium.
At home on Linux
Wayland and X11, your desktop’s notifications, a tray icon, start at login, and packages for your distribution.
40 colour schemes, dark and light
Harlo’s own, Catppuccin, Gruvbox, Nord, Solarized, Rosé Pine, Tokyo Night and more, with your fonts, text size and contrast.

Read what the agent did before you keep it

  • Only what changed, coloured by language. Comment on lines and send your comments to the agent as one message.
  • Undo a turn: its files go back as they were, all of them or one at a time.
  • Commit from the panel, with a message written for you if you like. Sign in to GitHub, GitLab or Forgejo in Settings.

It asks before it acts

  • Approvals: allow once, always allow, or deny and say what to do instead. A permission mode sets how much an agent may do without asking.
  • A sandbox for commands: what an agent runs can change the project, and can’t read your SSH keys, your sign-ins or Harlo’s own files. Leaving the sandbox asks you.
  • Questions too: when the agent needs a decision, it asks with choices you click.

The models you choose, your own included

  • Your plan or a key: sign in with your ChatGPT plan, or add a key for OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google and many more. A key is checked as you add it.
  • Local models: Harlo installs Ollama or llama.cpp, runs them, and gets models that fit your graphics card.
  • Any OpenAI-compatible server, your own included, and a different model for each session.

Plugins that wait for your yes

  • A plugin is a git repository that brings skills, commands, agents, MCP servers and tools written in JavaScript. Add one by its address, or from the list people have published.
  • Nothing runs unasked: the programs it starts, its code and what its code may reach are listed first, and an update that changes them asks again.
  • What you already have works: skills in .claude/skills and .agents/skills, your AGENTS.md, and MCP servers, sign-ins included.

They keep working when you close the window

Harlo’s own server runs your sessions in the background, on this computer or on another one you reach over SSH. The window, and your phone, follow along.

Schedules
Send a prompt or run a command at a time, every so often, or on a cron schedule, with the window closed.
Your other machines
A GPU box, a build server, a NAS: Harlo follows a Harlo server there and you work as you would here.
Subagents and memory
An agent hands parts of a job to subagents, and carries notes from one session to the next.
Coming from OpenCode
Your setup is copied over on the first start, and your sessions come along from Settings.

Free, open source, and yours
